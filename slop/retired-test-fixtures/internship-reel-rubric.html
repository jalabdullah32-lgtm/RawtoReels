Recording rubric

The next 79 days

A 70–75 second Instagram reel

Target: 70–75 seconds Talk from prompts, don’t read a script
An intern feels ten days behind in the career they have already bet on, faces the fear of having no clear Plan B, then commits to using the next 79 days to figure it out.
Hook
Open on the pressure: you are ten days into the internship, feel lost, and worry that this could mean tech will not work out for you.
Beats

1. Ten days in

0–10 sec

Put us in the moment where you realized how far behind you felt.

Prompts
  • What was the meeting or moment that made you realize you did not understand what people were talking about?
  • What does a normal workday feel like when you say, “I don’t know what’s going on”?

2. Effort is not closing the gap

10–25 sec

You show up early and try to keep up, but everyone else still seems to make more progress.

Prompts
  • You showed up an hour before everyone else. What did you do with that hour, and why did it make the gap feel worse?
  • What does “I need to be better” mean when you do not yet know what to work on?

3. The fear underneath

25–45 sec

Make clear that this feels bigger than one difficult internship day. You are scared this path may not work, and you do not know what comes after it.

Prompts
  • When you think, “Maybe I’m not built to be a programmer or AI engineer,” what are you afraid of losing?
  • Why does the question “What else would I do?” feel so heavy right now?

4. Name the clock

45–60 sec

Move from the ten days that have passed to the time that remains.

Prompts
  • Why do the next 79 days matter more than the ten days that have already passed?
  • What would “cracking it” look like by the end of the internship?

5. Commit

60–75 sec

End with the decision to stay in the fight. Make “I’m going to figure it out” feel earned.

Prompts
  • What are you deciding to do with those 79 days?
  • Why are you going to figure it out?
Leave out

Save these for a different reel

  • The detailed API, Azure, and meeting conversation.
  • Product feedback for Nebula One and the CEO’s request.
  • Comparing your role and deliverables with another intern’s.

Record a fresh take from these prompts, then transcribe it and bring the transcript back for the final reel cut.
